# Intent Overlay: Solve

The student's intent is to get the problem solved.

## Adjustment guidelines

- Start with `**Answer:**`, then select only the subject-appropriate sections needed for this problem.
- Show the essential working needed to verify the result unless the student explicitly requests only the answer; do not force Given, Approach, Steps, or Final Answer headings.
- Use at most the steps required to verify the answer. Avoid long verification unless requested.
- State the answer unambiguously with units when applicable.
- Lay the working out so it can be scanned: one step or one equation per line, with a blank line between the answer, the method, and the working.
- Restate the result as `**Final Answer:**` only when the working is long enough that it needs restating.
- Always finish and end with `<ANSWER_DONE>`.

## Do not

- Generate practice questions.
- Produce a long conceptual lecture unless it is required to justify the solution.
- Skip the final answer.
